Docs / Hardware

Supported hardware

Draft for #170. Placeholders marked TODO(#236/#…) wait on a decision. #236 tracks each one, and the second number is the decision's closed issue.

The private beta has eight unit slots (#116). Each slot is a unit that has to show something specific on real hardware before sndstation calls it supported. None has done that yet. Until a slot's issue closes, read every unit below as private beta, not yet verified: the platform is written for it, parts of it are measured, and the rest is a gap listed here.

Two units have no sound routes for the built-in card: the Powkiddy RGB30 (slot 4) and the TRIMUI Smart Pro (slot 6). Give each of them to a tester, not to an app author who needs sound to build an app.

The .deb sets a unit up as an sndstation system on six kinds of chip: RK3326, RK3566, H700, A133P, MT8167, and a Raspberry Pi with no desktop (packaging/make-deb.sh, handheld()). On any other machine it installs but changes nothing outside its own directory. SNDSTATION_SYSTEM=yes or no overrides the check. The eight slots are the units checked on real hardware. MT8167, the PlayStation Classic, is on the install list but has no slot (#185).

Whether the public beta covers every unit on those chips, or only the eight once they pass, is not decided yet (#116). The page says "the beta" for the eight.

Shorthand

Term Meaning
F3, F0 Device families from the survey in #100 §1. F3 is a gamepad handheld, with a d-pad and sticks. F0 is a headless board, with no panel.
OTP One-time-programmable memory on the SoC, written at manufacture. An RK3326 unit's SOC ID is read from it (unit-identity.md).
pad::layout() The SDK call that says where each control is on the body, in millimeters. A pad with no layout prints the standard names (gamepad-layout.md).
pixels/1 The display API every host offers a plugin: the plugin draws XRGB8888 pixels into the host's buffer (embedded-hardware.md).
gles/1, vulkan/1 Display APIs for a GPU, which #100 §1 proposes after pixels/1. The extension header does not define them yet.

What every slot has to show

Each unit's "must show" is its exit criterion. It is what a holder measures and reports (reporting.md). A unit with an open gap box has not yet shown it.

Unit by unit

1. Anbernic RG351V (RK3326), #126

  • Must show: --rt-bench reports a RESULT; the SOC ID row reads from OTP; the rk817 routes are learned by ear and survive a headphone plug; 48 kHz / 256 frames runs clean on hw, where plug underran.
  • Works, per the docs: the realtime audio thread. On this unit at 48 kHz / 512 frames with four competing threads, SCHED_FIFO 70 measured 90% headroom (rt-bench.md). Opening the card as hw:0,0 was clean at 48 kHz / 256 frames, where alsa-lib's plug over MMAP underran (audio-device.md, §3.3). A gamepad map exists (RG351V (ArkOS), with inverted analog axes in config/gamepads.json).
  • Open gap: no body layout (pad::layout()), so the pad prints the standard names. The slot's report is also owed.
  • Known not to work: nothing recorded.

2. R36S Blue and R40XX (RK3326), #127

One slot, two boxes.

  • Must show: the two units get different SOC IDs and separate audio.json entries; the R36S body's controls and layout are right.
  • Works, per the docs: the two units already have different ids on the ABOUT page, read from OTP though their device trees are alike: RK3326-URKC08010-A599D7394C5B58B6 (R40XX) and RK3326-URKC08010-4FE23CAAD163F02F (R36S Blue) (unit-identity.md). A gamepad map for the R36S and R36S Plus exists in config/gamepads.json.
  • Open gaps: no body layout; no rt-bench result; the slot's report.
  • Known not to work: nothing recorded.

3. XU10 (RK3326), #128

  • Must show: pad::layout() matches the body within the stated 2 mm; rear shoulders and the edge Menu are placed correctly (the face gap of #100 §1, on real hardware).
  • Works, per the docs: a gamepad map and a layout, measured from photos of every side and scaled to the unit's 130 mm height. Its shoulders are on the back and its Menu is the G on the right edge (gamepad-layout.md, config/gamepads.json).
  • Open gaps: no rt-bench result; the slot's report.
  • Known not to work: nothing recorded.

4. Powkiddy RGB30 (RK3566), #129

  • Must show: the built-in card is picked over HDMI's card 0; the RK3566 rt-bench (CONFIG_RT_GROUP_SCHED) finding reproduces or is closed.
  • Works, per the docs: the card choice. Card 0 is HDMI and the speaker codec is card 1, so sndstation takes the first card that is not a display output (audio-device.md, §3.3). A bench run on kernel 7.0.2, booted with rt_group_sched=0, was clean through 90% with four competing threads and failed at 95% (rt-bench.md). A gamepad map exists.
  • Open gaps: no body layout; no routes for the built-in card (there is a card choice but no codec table); the slot's report.
  • Known not to work: realtime scheduling is refused for a login session on a kernel with CONFIG_RT_GROUP_SCHED unless it is booted with rt_group_sched=0 (rt-bench.md, "Allowing it"). Its /proc/asound has no per-stream status, so the bench reports access=unknown buffer=unknown. See known-issues.md.

5. Anbernic RG40XX H (H700), #130

Also stands for the RG CubeXX.

  • Must show: the rings light in the shipped slot order without root; the h616 route table and the speaker amp switch play where test_h616_route says; SDL's ALSA stream stays off this card (the sun6i_dma_interrupt panic).
  • Works, per the docs: the stick-light rings are verified. On an RG40XX H under a ROCKNIX kernel, slots 0-7 are the right ring and 8-15 the left. Each slot took its own color, checked by eye (stick-leds.md). The h616 codec reports the jack and leaves the speaker on, so its speaker switch is its whole routing; this is tested headless (--fake-audio h616, tests/test_h616_route.cpp), not on the unit (launcher.md, Audio).
  • Open gaps: a button map of its own; a body layout; the slot's report. The button map has to land before the developer beta starts. Today there is only the speculative RG28xx (more?) entry, so the unit cannot get past the first screen.
  • Known not to work: the button map above. SDL's ALSA stream under PREEMPT_RT panics the kernel; sndstation uses its own stream, so this only shows if SNDSTATION_AUDIO_STREAM=sdl is set. See known-issues.md.

6. TRIMUI Smart Pro (A133P), #131

Also stands for the Brick.

  • Must show: the install recognizes the unit and sets it up to boot into the launcher; the layout's shoulders (on top) are placed; a plugin drawing through pixels/1 alone is usable.
  • Works, per the docs: the install's handheld allowlist includes the sun50i-a100 device tree, and this is the only unit that uses it (packaging/make-deb.sh). Two gamepad maps exist (one for Knulli, one for the unit's own image) and a layout, taken from the maker's front-on photo scaled to 80 mm, accurate to about 2-3 mm. The photo does not show the top edge, so the shoulders are printed, not placed (gamepad-layout.md, config/gamepads.json).
  • Open gaps: no routes for the built-in card (nothing for the A133P yet); no rt-bench result; the slot's report.
  • Known not to work: sound from the built-in card, until routes exist. See known-issues.md.

7. Anbernic RG35XX SP (H700), #132

The only clamshell.

  • Must show: on the sndstation rootfs, the lid closes and opens through evdev gpio-keys-lid, and the lid policy acts on it. On the stock BSP image, the hallkey attribute is read open and shut, and becomes the first entry in config/deviceevents.json.
  • Works, per the docs: on the sndstation rootfs (a mainline kernel) the lid is evdev gpio-keys-lid, value 1 for closed, and the lid policy (suspend by default) is applied to logind at boot (device-events.md). The Realtek Bluetooth chip fails to answer on about half of boots; a boot service rebinds it (launcher.md, "Bluetooth at boot").
  • Open gaps: a button map of its own (needed before the developer beta, as for slot 5); a body layout; no rt-bench result; a lid entry; the slot's report. config/deviceevents.json ships empty, so the beta is the lid's measurement.
  • Known not to work: the button map above. The lid is not read through hallkey on any unit, because no polarity has been measured. See known-issues.md.
  • Two images: the sndstation rootfs, where the lid is evdev, and a stock BSP image, where hallkey can be read: TODO(#236/#124).

8. Raspberry Pi 4, OS Lite (BCM2711), #133

The headless case, with no pad and no backlight.

  • Must show: the install takes the no-desktop branch; with no pad and no backlight, nothing claims a panel or geometry; vulkan/1 can be offered beside pixels/1 and gles/1.
  • Works, per the docs: the install treats a Pi, like any board with a device tree, as a handheld only when no desktop is installed (packaging/make-deb.sh). The unit id comes from the firmware's serial in the device tree, for example BCM2711-100000001A2B3C4D (unit-identity.md).
  • Open gaps: no rt-bench result; the slot's report.
  • Known not to work: nothing recorded.

Probably supported

The eight slots are the units someone has agreed to verify. They are not the limit of what runs. sndstation gates on the SoC, not the model, and on whether a desktop is installed: packaging/make-deb.sh sets a machine up as a unit when the device tree's compatible matches one of

compatible Slot that covers it
rockchip,rk3326 1, 2, 3
rockchip,rk3566 4
allwinner,sun50i-h700 5, 7
allwinner,sun50i-a100 6 (the A133P's device tree calls itself sun50i-a100)
mediatek,mt8167 none
any other, with no desktop installed 8 (any Pi, not only the 4); an SBC or a TV box on Armbian's Minimal or Server images, with no slot behind it

An S905X TV box on Armbian (amlogic,meson-gxl, HDMI, a USB gamepad) installs this way; it has no slot and has not been verified.

So a unit this page never names probably works, if it is on one of those SoCs. RK3326 is the broadest case: src/rk3326_otp.h comes from reading "18+ retail RK3326 handhelds from multiple manufacturers (Anbernic, Powkiddy, ...)", and the launcher's identity, audio, and realtime paths key off the SoC rather than the model. mediatek,mt8167 installs with no slot behind it at all.

What "probably" is doing in that sentence is the gamepad. The database in config/gamepads.json holds four layouts—ps_classic, trimui_smart_pro, xbox, xu10. A unit whose pad matches none of them gets the fallback: it runs, and some buttons are in the wrong place. That is the expected failure for an unnamed unit, and it is a config fix, not a port.
