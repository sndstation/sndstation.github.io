Code of Conduct

Draft for #140. Placeholders marked TODO(#236/#…) wait on a decision. #236 tracks each one, and the second number is the decision's closed issue.

sndstation adopts the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 as its code of conduct. It is adopted by reference: the linked text is the code, and this page does not copy it.

In short

  • Be respectful. Disagree with an idea without attacking the person.
  • Harassment, discrimination, threats, and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable.
  • It applies in every space the project runs: issues, pull requests, review comments, and wherever the project's chat lives. It also applies when someone speaks for the project in public.
  • Maintainers may remove or edit contributions, and may ban a person, to uphold it.

The Covenant has the full standards and the enforcement guidelines. Where this summary and the Covenant differ, the Covenant wins.

Reporting

Proposal, for the maintainers to accept or change. Reports are read by at least two people, not a single maintainer. A report about one of the readers is handled by the others, and that person takes no part in it. Reports are kept confidential: the people who read them share the details with no one else, except where a decision needs it.

Enforcement

The people who read reports decide the response, using the Covenant's enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). They tell the reporter what was decided. TODO(#236/#191): who they are and how a report about one of them is handled in practice.
