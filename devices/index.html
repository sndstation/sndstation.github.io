Sources and what was left out

Handhelds: Retro Handhelds community spreadsheet (591 devices): SoC, cores, clocks, GPU, RAM, OS, connectivity (used for USB host: "OTG" or "USB-A" listed ⇒ ✓) and average price. Kept only aarch64: __DROPPED__.

Phones · Tablets & laptops · Chromebooks · Boards & TV boxes (split by each postmarketOS page's own device type): postmarketOS device matrix (the wiki now redirects to wiki.nura.eco): supported aarch64 devices with their category (community / testing / downstream) and whether Audio and USB-OTG work. Chipsets read from each device page. Prices for these are estimates (used, by year and class) and marked est.

Apple: 91 iPhones, iPads and MacBooks from A11 to A20 Pro and M1 to M5 Max, plus the postmarketOS-supported A7–A11 devices. Current models use current US retail; older ones use an estimated used price. Apple chip scores are scaled from Geekbench 6 single-core per GHz; GPU GFLOPS are estimated as cores × 128 ALUs × 2 × clock. Tiers: M1/M2 MacBooks are M1 (Asahi Linux with working speakers); M3 MacBooks are M2 (Asahi support official since Sep 2026, audio works, no GPU driver yet); M4, M5 and the A18 Pro MacBook Neo are S3 (macOS only until Asahi catches up); A7–A11 iOS devices without a port are S4 (checkm8 can boot a custom kernel); A12+ iPhones and all iPads are S0 (locked boot).

Music players: HiFi Oasis DAP database (134 players, with SoC, price, year and screen). 35 players on 32-bit ARM or MIPS chips (classic iPods, Ingenic-based players) are left out; the aarch64 ones, including iPod touch 6th/7th gen, are in.

Music hardware: inMusic units confirmed by the sndstation team, current US retail.

The earlier sources (handhelds.miraheze wiki, chromebook.wiki, the Android fandom list) are replaced by these. x86 and 32-bit devices are no longer included.

How the scores are calculated

Power Index (multi-core) and the single-core index: cores × per-core speed (core type × clock), RK3326 (4× Cortex-A35 @ 1.5 GHz) = 100. kFLOPs/sample: fastest core's peak FP32 per audio sample at 48 kHz. GPU GFLOPS: cores × FP32 lanes × 2 × clock.

Two matching families, all RK3326 = 100. Capability (no price): Power Index = raw multi-core; Audio Index = single0.6·multi0.4; GFX Index = multi0.7·GPU0.3 (GPU part = GFLOPS ÷ 21 × 100). Value (per dollar, a $40 RK3326 R4 handheld = 100): Audio DSP = 40 × Audio Index × tier × rt factor × verification ÷ price; GFX DSP = 40 × GFX Index × tier × verification ÷ price. rt factor = (headroom ÷ 90) × (512 ÷ frames)0.25, so RK3326's measured 90% @ 512 = 1; a 256-frame unit gets ×1.19. From your measured rt-bench where present, otherwise a per-chip estimate.

Verification: the Audio DSP score is multiplied by how sure we are the unit runs sndstation with a USB host. ×1 confirmed (measured rt-bench, repo evidence, or confirmed by hand); ×0.85 strong (spec says open boot + USB host, or in the repo with USB host unconfirmed); ×0.6 likely (chip family boots, USB likely, needs verification); ×0.3 unknown. Partial scores are shown as ~N. The default view includes the "should work" units; switch the filter to "Verified only" to hide them.

Known limits: core speeds and GPU GFLOPS are estimates from core type × clock, not benchmarks; Apple GPU and A20 Pro figures are guesses; 39 S1 handhelds are S1 only because of their chip family (no repo or bench evidence); handheld prices from the sheet are often 2020–22 launch prices; every Phone / Chromebook / Board price and most Apple prices are estimates.

Speculative ports guesses every per-dollar number for a full theoretical port: readiness jumps to ×1 if you can boot your own OS (locked devices stay put), verification is assumed (×1), and the rt-bench stays the per-chip estimate. Audio DSP and GFX DSP show the native score with ×Y beside it, the ± and the bars follow the ceiling, and the sndstation column shows the ceiling tier multiplier (→ ×c). Y = 1 ÷ (readiness × verification) for open-boot devices, i.e. how much the same hardware would score if sndstation were fully ported to it. Sorting uses the ceiling while the toggle is on.
